Services

Public offers are intentionally focused: assess the system, ship one workflow, or train the team so adoption survives after the session.

AI Readiness Audit

Best for founders, operators, and leadership teams who need to know what to automate, what to avoid, and what to do first.

  • 2-hour discovery or full-day audit
  • Tool, data, process, and risk inventory
  • Opportunity map with effort and impact scoring
  • One-page executive summary plus roadmap

Implementation Sprint

Best when the team already has a painful workflow and needs a usable prototype, not another strategy document.

  • One workflow selected and scoped tightly
  • Automation, agent, or assistant prototype
  • Human review and escalation design
  • Handover guide and operating checklist

Workshops & Training

Best for teams that need practical AI literacy across business, sales, marketing, operations, and management.

  • Executive briefings
  • Hands-on AI workflow labs
  • Prompting and judgment practice
  • English, French, or Spanish delivery

Working style

The point is not to make AI look impressive. The point is to install workflows people can actually use, inspect, and improve.

1. DiagnoseMap the workflow, users, tools, risks, and constraints.
2. PrototypeShip the smallest useful version with clear human control.
3. TrainMake the team capable of using and questioning the system.
4. MeasureTrack hours saved, quality improved, or response time reduced.
